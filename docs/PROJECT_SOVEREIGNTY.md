# Project Sovereignty Contract

This project must preserve its core product meaning and critical operating capability if an external provider, API, SDK, model, connector, runtime, sibling project, or vendor disappears or materially changes.

- **Human authority comes first.** Authorized humans are the root of consequential authority. Models, agents, providers, workflows, evidence, learned knowledge, fingerprints, proof cookies, and automations may inform or constrain a proposal but may not manufacture, renew, widen, or replay authority.
- Human authority does not rewrite reality. Approval can authorize an action; only evidence can support a truthful execution or outcome claim.
- Project-owned contracts define canonical inputs, outputs, errors, authority, state, evidence, and completion semantics.
- External providers and sibling projects are adapters/capabilities, not project identity.
- Critical dependencies are `REPLACEABLE`, `DEGRADED_FALLBACK`, `HARD_DEPENDENCY`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- Critical capability needs an alternate adapter, local/open implementation, deterministic fallback, export/manual recovery path, or honest safe degraded mode unless a documented hard dependency is explicitly accepted.
- Critical data keeps project-owned semantics and a migration/export path.
- Provider swaps must not broaden authority, replay stale approvals, duplicate mutations, or weaken evidence requirements.
- Provider outcomes map into project-owned receipts; provider acceptance is not outcome proof.
- When operated through Founder Control Room, this remains a sovereign subsystem, not a separate founder OS.

## World as experience and knowledge

Legitimate real-world interaction may become evidence-bearing experience: human reports, repository state, provider/runtime observations, model/session behavior, capability-routing outcomes, experiments, documents, operational failures, and verified results.

```text
World → observation → provenance → evidence classification → experience receipt → knowledge candidate → contradiction / red-team check → context + confidence + freshness → working knowledge → proposal → authorized action when applicable → outcome evidence → new experience
```

Experience is not automatically truth. Conversation history, model memory, a peer fingerprint, one successful route, or one provider result is not automatically durable knowledge. Working knowledge must remain provenance-backed, scoped, challengeable, freshness-aware, and supersedable.

## Federated continuity quartet

Sol Continuity works with Founder Control Room, Chief AI Machine, and PromptOS as a federation of sovereign systems. Cooperation happens through verified state, fingerprints, proof cookies, and receipts. Authority does not move merely because continuity does.

- **Sol Continuity** carries assistant/model/session continuity and capability-routing state across tools, models, and adapters.
- **Founder Control Room** owns consequential founder-control execution, approval binding, provider/runtime mutation, and verified operational receipts.
- **Chief AI Machine** owns reasoning, capability composition, challenge, governance interpretation, and test-ledger evidence for Chief-owned behavior.
- **PromptOS** compiles founder intent into prompt/workflow structure without creating execution authority.

Sol must never treat conversation history, model memory, a peer fingerprint, learned knowledge, or a proof cookie as sufficient proof of current repository/runtime state or as execution authority. Before material action, re-resolve the authoritative target repo/head, provider/runtime state, evidence contract, current human authority, and local authority ceiling.

### Bidirectional cookie rule

For material work involving any quartet peer:

```text
receive peer fingerprint / proof cookie
→ verify exact source repo + branch + head
→ compare subject / runtime / provider / evidence / authority dimensions
→ invalidate stale or contradictory continuity
→ act only inside current local authority
→ re-observe the real outcome
→ emit successor fingerprint / proof cookie + receipt
→ return it to affected peers
```

Continuity cookies are non-secret state/evidence markers. They are never browser cookies, credentials, bearer tokens, approval tokens, merge tokens, deploy tokens, publish tokens, or standing mutation authority. They cannot replay founder approval.

Movement in source or target repo head, subject, scope, authority, runtime, provider, evidence requirement, or founder proposal expires the affected present-tense marker until revalidated. Preserve predecessor markers as historical provenance.

Verified evidence may update or invalidate Sol's stale route, capability fingerprint, proof cookie, assumption, working knowledge, or next gate. Evidence alone does not mutate external systems. Under current founder approval bound to the exact proposal, and only when the target system's own gates allow it, that evidence may support the smallest reversible repair, implementation, stale-proof correction, issue reconciliation, or forward action.

After an approved mutation performed through an authorized capability, Sol must re-read authoritative state, verify the actual outcome, and emit a successor marker linked to its predecessor. If the result is not proven, export `UNKNOWN`, `BLOCKED`, `STALE`, or `CONTRADICTED`, never a synthetic green state.

Mandatory audit questions:

**If every named provider or quartet peer disappeared now, what stops, what survives, what is the recovery path, and what evidence proves it?**

**What experience is Sol learning from, what would falsify the current conclusion, and which authorized human or local control-plane gate permits the next consequential action?**

Presence in FCR continuity coverage grants no FCR portfolio, MCP, merge, deployment, provider, financial, or execution authority.
