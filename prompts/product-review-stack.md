# SolContinuity product review stack

Use this order for major product decisions.

1. **First principles:** What failure must the user survive? Which assumptions are physical, protocol, vendor, or habit?
2. **Socrates:** What do we know, infer, and merely repeat? What evidence would reverse the decision?
3. **Unlearn:** Remove inherited architecture that does not serve the failure path.
4. **80/20:** Select the smallest feature that proves the central grant claim.
5. **Y Combinator:** Identify the specific user, urgent pain, current workaround, and observable adoption behavior.
6. **Hormozi:** Clarify the valuable outcome, likelihood of success, time delay, and user effort. Do not inflate promises.
7. **Human:** Can a developer understand, recover, and act without decoding internal jargon?
8. **Antiadvice:** List common recommendations that would make this project broader, more centralized, or harder to verify.
9. **Lindymode:** Prefer portable formats, open protocols, plain HTTP/JSON, small interfaces, and replaceable providers.
10. **Redteam:** Attack operator independence, response collusion, stale caches, poisoned RPCs, frontend compromise, and evidence tampering.
11. **L99:** Record authority, current state, evidence, rollback, and compounding value.
12. **OODA:** Observe, orient, decide, act, verify, and loop.
13. **TRUTHMODE:** Label verified, inferred, unknown, and blocked claims before publishing.
14. **FutureYOU:** Choose work that relieves the bottleneck, compounds reuse, increases evidence, preserves control, and shortens time-to-proof.
