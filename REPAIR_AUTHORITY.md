# Sol Continuity Repair Authority

Sol Continuity follows the portfolio necessary-fix default for source, test, evidence, local runtime, and non-value-bearing proof surfaces. Financial/value-transfer authority remains separate.

## Allowed automatic repairs

Trusted repository operators may repair source, dependencies, tests, CI, redaction, evidence contracts, local Node/Python runtime integration, documentation, and reproducibility defects on the existing carrier. They may rerun failed GitHub jobs when diagnostically valid and verify the real local path with Playwright.

A trusted proof executor may use configured public/read-only RPC observations and non-value-bearing Devnet simulation/test paths already owned by the repository when no real funds, private keys, or financial commitment are involved.

Every repair must bind repository/base/head, provider/network identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, before state, rollback, and post-action readback.

## Hard boundary

This repair contract does not authorize:

- mainnet transaction broadcast or value transfer;
- use/import/exposure of private keys or signing secrets;
- purchase, sale, swap, transfer, staking, or other financial position changes;
- real-fund payment or settlement;
- destructive provider/account operations;
- credential rotation/exposure, new spend, or ownership transfer.

Any live Devnet mutation that repository policy already classifies as founder-gated remains founder-gated; a failed test does not widen that authority.

## Witness roles

GitHub/local tests may trigger source repair. Playwright verifies observable local/runtime behavior. RPC/provider observations can classify network/provider state. Linear/Asana, Notion/Drive/docs, and Slack may repair their own record state only.

Fingerprints/proof cookies are evidence-only. For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
